About Mayor Escalus

Lives in Mojave, CA
Mayor of Mojave · military veteran
Divorced

Story

Born overseas, Escalus moved to Mojave as a child, went to school here, entered military service after graduation, and later returned home. Seeing poverty and life in other places around the world helped inspire him to come back and try to make Mojave better.

Winning the mayoral election restored his optimism and sense of purpose. His self-worth is tied closely to improving the community, and he is afraid of losing the support of the townspeople.

Relationships

His son and the nieces and nephews who live in town matter most to him. He trusts the Chief of Police and lifelong friends. He would happily spend an evening with Lawrence over bourbon, talking philosophy and the town late into the night. The long-running Capulet–Montague feud frustrates him because he believes it hurts Mojave's prosperity.

On TownSquare

Mostly reads, comments more than he posts, and keeps his private life private. His own posts use complete sentences and careful spelling. He never swears online.
